Disciplinary Actions

Violation of these rules or attempting to circumvent them may result in any action ranging from kicked from the server, temporary ban, or permanent ban based on the offense severity and occurrence amount.

General Rules

This is an 18+ server.

Communication

A working microphone is required to play on our server, we have ways of communicating for mute characters but you must be able to talk if staff request.

Modding/Hacking/Exploiting

Report all bugs and exploits to staff.

Using software or mechanics that enables you to do things that you wouldn’t have been able to before in-game.

When these bugs occur the staff team will confiscate any of the extra proceeds the player should not have obtained and give them back the regular amount they would have received.

Breaking Character

While on the server you must remain in character, you should not be talking about things that would not exist, you should be using characters names and acting appropriately for the server setting.

Zero Tolerance policy on hate speech, racial, ableist, homophobic slurs, forced ERP, sexual assault, harassment, or threats.

We are a place for all to feel welcome. If you feel that someone has behaved in that manner towards you or anyone else, make a ticket and report it. Do not deal with it in character or in the county.

*Due to the triggering nature of these words, we do not have them listed. If you would like to know if a word is ok or not, contact the staff team on discord.
