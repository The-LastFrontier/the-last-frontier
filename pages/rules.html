County Laws & Regulations

The Last Frontier • 1880
Know the law. Respect the county.

01 — General Rules

Minimum Age

This is an 18+ server.

Communication

A working microphone is required to play on our server. We have ways of communicating for mute characters, but you must be able to talk if staff request it.

Breaking Character

While on the server, you must remain in character. Do not discuss things that would not exist in the setting. Use character names and act appropriately for the server's time period.

Harassment

If someone asks you to not message them on any platform, stop. Do not take out your OOC feelings on someone IC. If staff is contacted about harassment you will receive one warning to stop. If you do not stop you will be banned.

Zero Tolerance Policy!

Hate speech, racial or ableist slurs, homophobic slurs, forced ERP, sexual assault, harassment, and threats are prohibited.

We want everyone to feel welcome. If you experience or witness this behavior, open a ticket and report it to staff. Do not attempt to handle it in character or in the county.

For questions about language not explicitly listed in the policy, contact staff through Discord.

02 — Modding, Hacking & Exploiting

Report Exploits

Report all bugs and exploits to staff.

Unfair Advantages

Using software or exploiting game mechanics to perform actions that would not normally be possible in-game is prohibited.

Exploit Corrections

Staff will confiscate improperly obtained proceeds and restore the amount the player should have received through regular gameplay.

Disciplinary Actions

Violating these rules or attempting to circumvent them may result in a kick, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on the severity of the offense and the number of occurrences.
