County Laws & Regulations

The Last Frontier • 1880
Know the law. Respect the county.

01 — General Rules

Minimum Age

This is an 18+ server.

Communication

A working microphone is required to play on our server. We have ways of communicating for mute characters, but you must be able to talk if staff request it.

Breaking Character

While on the server, you must remain in character. Do not discuss things that would not exist in the setting. Use character names and act appropriately for the server's time period.

Harassment

If someone asks you to not message them on any platform, stop. Do not take out your OOC feelings on someone IC. If staff is contacted about harassment you will receive one warning to stop. If you do not stop you will be banned.

Zero Tolerance Policy!

Hate speech, racial or ableist slurs, homophobic slurs, forced ERP, sexual assault, harassment, and threats are prohibited.

We want everyone to feel welcome. If you experience or witness this behavior, open a ticket and report it to staff. Do not attempt to handle it in character or in the county.

For questions about language not explicitly listed in the policy, contact staff through Discord.

02 — Modding, Hacking & Exploiting

Report Exploits

Report all bugs and exploits to staff.

Unfair Advantages

Using software or exploiting game mechanics to perform actions that would not normally be possible in-game is prohibited.

Exploit Corrections

Staff will confiscate improperly obtained proceeds and restore the amount the player should have received through regular gameplay.

03 — Roleplay Guidelines

Character Names

Characters must be named appropriately for this time Era 1900 (This includes things that are trying to be hidden in context like Han Job, Mike Rotch, Etc.) and cannot be from history, movies, books, games, etc. You can make a reference but you can’t be the famous gunslinger Doc Holiday. The same goes for tribes and names for natives. You will have to change it if your name does not meet these simple requirements.

When on the server, your steam name must be something unique to you. It cannot be any variation of body, unknown, stranger, person, no one, guy, girl, local, etc.... it must be something unique that staff can pinpoint to easily assist you.

Addressing staff IC/ in State

Please do not approach staff in the state regarding OOC OR IC issues. If you need assistance, open a ticket, or use the request staff button in your player menu. Do not approach Staff while they are streaming to fix a problem, if you need to talk about addressing staff members, refer to “writing a letter to the state officials”.

**Do not DM Staff directly for any server issues. Utilize the ticket system for all questions or concerns**

Stories and the past

A question that’s always asked is what happens to my story and my character? Do I know people or not? Our team has decided that you may remember things you’ve done in your past but that those involved are vague characters unless you have agreed with that player to know each other. If you abuse this to place a person in a situation where they do not have a choice as to what is going on, this counts as powergaming. If you are found to be doing so to create drama for no reason other than to create drama then you will be found to be breaking the server rule of outside drama stays where it is. If you have any questions please reach out.

I.E Nate, played by Jannings, was a deputy and he arrested Clyde, played by Klown, for moonshining. Now in New Frontier, Klown has decided this is a different version of Clyde, so now in Nate’s story it’s just that he arrested someone for moonshining.

Altering Appearance/ Changing name

Instantly growing hair is not acceptable. For example, if your character suddenly goes from bald to long hair, you would have to RP that it’s a wig.

Fake IDs are available in game to be able to give to the law or show when needed for a fake name other than your real one.

Medical RP and Injuries

Not roleplaying the consequences of your injuries is considered another case of FailRP. If you are healed by a doctor or a native shaman, follow their instructions since we trust those players to be able to guide you on what to do.

If you are healing yourself, try to be as realistic as possible with your injuries and how to act with your character about it. Think about those as great occasions to roleplay something different, that can lead to awesome interactions and stories.

Even if your character is at full health, that doesn’t mean that he’s fine. You can roleplay an immense amount of injuries and consequences, like wound infections, fever, coughing, flu, allergies, and the list goes on and on. Everything brings just more roleplay to the table!

Also when you are downed and do not have full conversations like you are normal. You can talk like you are injured, make noises etc. Use /me or /status to show what injuries there are to help with medical procedures.

Major infectious diseases that would cause an outbreak are reserved for staff creation. Critical illnesses to include but not limited to, tuberculosis, gangrene, scarlet fever, cholera etc are reserved for the permanent end of a character.

Suicide/Pregnancy loss RP

Suicide/Pregnancy loss RP is not allowed. It can be seriously harmful and triggering to people IRL and will not be tolerated.

Moving items between Characters/ Housing

You may not transfer items, assets, or money to your other characters through any means. Things may be given out to others but cannot come back to you. You may only own one physical house per player, you may have instance house interiors for your other characters. This is to make sure there are enough physical houses for everyone to start.

Wagon Usage

Using any command or menu to make your wagon disappear during a hostile situation is considered an abuse of a mechanic. Also, you can not use it to skip transportation of goods via wagon by fleeing the wagon and pulling it out when you make it to your destination. To help negate this wagons and carts are only able to be called for at the stable in towns.

Supernatural Roleplay

Supernatural roleplay is allowed to the extent of common myths, legends, and folklore that was relevant to the time and the cinematic universe of Red Dead Redemption. This includes ghosts, vampires, werewolves, witches, etc.

This does not mean that you can play a werewolf or vampire as your character, but you can set up scenarios such as bodies drained of blood, or a voodoo cult, but you may not use “supernatural powers” to attempt to one up another player, ie powergame

Perma

Only you can decide to permanently kill your character outside of the judicial system, no player can force you to permanently kill a character. If you perma, this is a final decision and can not be undone. Please consider your decision carefully before finalizing. You may reach out to the staff team for help planning your character’s death. If an individual is sentenced to death by the judicial system, this is not something we take lightly as it is a very big thing to perma a character and would take a lot of crime, options may be discussed with the staff team and judge to help plan for the characters story prior to the trial.

04 — Terms to know

Revenge RP

Killing a player directly after they have killed you or trying to do something back to someone for no true reason other than because they did something to you.

RDM/VDM

The acts of randomly killing with or without a vehicle, without proper initiation, for no purpose other than to “troll” and be a problem for those wishing to actually roleplay, if you are just walking up to people and shooting them in the head then that's RDM.

Combat logging/Respawning

F8 quitting, or pressing E while downed, during a hostile scene/interaction because you don’t want to die/lose/get jailed/etc. You should wait at least 30 minutes after being involved in a situation before leaving the server. IF YOU HAVE AN EMERGENCY at the time of the hostile event and have to leave the server, open a ticket and let the staff team know so they can reach out to the parties involved.

Meta-gaming

Using out of character information to help you in-character, such as crucial information on a gang via the general channel in Discord, twitch streams etc. Do not be in someone's stream while you are on the server. If you are seen in their stream this will be considered attempted meta.

Power-gaming

Trying to gain advantage over any player you see and/or acting more powerful than you are. Abusing or using knowledge of mechanics or just making up rp to get an advantage or a "win".

Examples: - Forcing others into your rp by not giving them any way to remove themselves from the situation at hand other than by being killed or requesting staff intervention. - Using /me to gain an unfair advantage in a situation IE “/me takes all ammo and knocks you out” while not actually doing the actions. - Waiting until someone enters a menu of some sort and rob them then, making it so they have no physical way to react, they can’t raise hands, turn around etc

Fear/Value of life

All characters must operate with some level of fear for their lives. While your characters may not truly die, they must act as if their character is subject to fear due to the possibility of being injured or losing their life. You must take into account all repercussions while your life's on the line, you can't be a hero every time. However, if your character would reasonably be able to fight back against those attacking, then that is allowed.

Value the life of others. If a criminal has a hostage you need to value the life of the hostage. Don’t go charging in head first.

Fail- RP/Unrealistic actions

Fail roleplay is when a person fails to roleplay properly, whether that be going down an unrealistic route in history, or intentionally disrupting the roleplay by trolling

NLR (New life rule)

When you are downed and have to respawn, your memory of what happened in the 30 minutes prior to you respawning is close to nothing. Someone involved in the RP may “jog” your memory of the events but you should not be actively seeking it out.

Character Bleed

Allowing your OOC emotions to break through IC does nothing productive for RP. Be considerate and work towards conflict resolution and compromise. Get staff to mediate if you need it.

05 — Crime Guidelines

Server Restart and Crime

No crime may be committed 15 minutes before OR after a restart (storm) , this is in order to ensure that any criminal actions can have sufficient roleplay.

If you are currently in an RP situation when the restart happens, you are expected to log back in and return to RP, if unable, notify Staff and the parties involved.

Hogtie

You can break out with your knife if you are lassoed, but once your hands are tied up you can no longer break out. You are not to give up while hogtied to just leave the rp. Choosing the option to “Give Up” causes your character to go down and you will have to go through medical RP. If you respawn while still hogtied in an RP scenario that counts as combat logging. You may try to break out of thrown bolas until tied up.

You must initiate verbal contact with the player before engaging in violent actions.

Hostages

You have 15 minutes from taking a hostage to start your job. Once a scenario is started you will need to complete said scenario within a reasonable amount of time. Please be considerate of other people’s time and rp.

You may not kill a compliant hostage unless deemed absolutely necessary in the RP scenario. These rules are in place to encourage people to comply when being taken hostage for a job. You must try your hardest to obtain a hostage that is not associated with you, if none can be found you may take someone associated with you hostage, but if found that the hostage is involved in the robbery they may be arrested and charged.

You may not take a LEO or Doctor hostage if there’s less than 2 on duty.

Disciplinary Actions

Violating these rules or attempting to circumvent them may result in a kick, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on the severity of the offense and the number of occurrences.
